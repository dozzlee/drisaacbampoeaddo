# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Family, friends, colleagues, CLOGSAG members, public servants, and members of the Ghanaian public visiting to remember Dr Isaac Atteh Bampoe Addo, understand his public-service legacy, view photographs, and contribute a tribute.

## Product Purpose

Create a dignified permanent tribute that records Dr Bampoe Addo's life, leadership, and service. Success means visitors can immediately understand who he was, move through his legacy without friction on any device, and confidently share a memory of their own.

## Brand Personality

Solemn, resolute, and personal. The experience should feel like a carefully prepared memorial programme: respectful enough for bereaved family and colleagues, but warm enough to celebrate a full life and enduring public legacy.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic corporate landing page, a cheerful lifestyle aesthetic, decorative funeral clichés, text placed over a visually busy face, disconnected image panels, and ornamental effects that compete with the subject. The supplied interface references inform structure, controls, and precision, not their SaaS subject matter.

## Design Principles

- Keep the person, not the interface, at the centre of every composition.
- Use contrast and restraint to create dignity, then let the established red carry emphasis.
- Make the first screen immediately legible, especially on a phone.
- Treat every contribution action as an invitation, not a sales conversion.
- Preserve the record: names, dates, achievements, quotations, and photographs remain clear and easy to navigate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction standards. Maintain readable body sizes, visible keyboard focus, descriptive image and control labels, touch targets of at least 44px, reduced-motion alternatives, and layouts that remain usable with text zoom and narrow screens.
